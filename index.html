Privacy Policy for Proyas

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Proyas ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Proyas mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our educational and exam preparation services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a personalized HSC and Admission Test preparation experience, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account & Authentication Data: When you register or sign in via Firebase Authentication, we collect your unique user identifier (UID) and email address.
  • Personal Profile Information: Information you voluntarily provide and edit in your profile, including your full name, phone number, birthdate, gender, residential address, and HSC batch.
  • Academic & Exam Performance Data: We record your exam submissions, selected answers, accuracy statistics, subject-wise progress, total questions attempted, and scores across Practice, Question Bank, Free Exam, and Subscription tests.
  • Subscription & Course Enrollment Data: Records of courses and exam batches you subscribe to within the App.
  • User Feedback & Communications: Messages, inquiries, and feedback you submit through our in-app "Tell Us More" / Comment Box feature, along with your UID and submission timestamp.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly to operate and improve the Proyas App:

  • Service Delivery: To authenticate your account, display your personalized dashboard, track exam countdown schedules, and grant access to subscribed exam batches.
  • Progress Tracking & Analytics: To calculate your exam results, accuracy rates, and subject-wise performance summaries.
  • Customer Support: To respond to questions, feedback, or support requests submitted via the Comment Box.
  • App Maintenance & Security: To monitor system stability, prevent unauthorized access or exam content misuse, and improve user experience.

3. Data Storage & Third-Party Services

  • Cloud Infrastructure: Your account credentials, profile details, exam progress, and feedback messages are securely stored using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore).
  • Data Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Data is shared only with essential service providers (such as Google Firebase) strictly to host and operate the App, or when required by applicable law in Bangladesh.

4. Data Retention & Your Rights

  • Access and Updates: You can view and update your personal details (such as your name, phone number, birthdate, address, and HSC batch) at any time directly within the Profile section of the App.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You may request the deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us through the in-app support feature or via email. Upon verification, we will remove your personal profile and progress records from our active databases within a reasonable timeframe.

5. Children’s Privacy

Proyas is designed for HSC and university admission candidates. If you are under 18 years of age, please ensure you use the App and provide personal information with the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security rules and access controls (including Firebase Security Rules) so that your personal profile and exam progress records can only be accessed by your authenticated account. However, no digital transmission or cloud storage method is 100% immune to risk.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as we add new features to Proyas. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
